Northwind Analytics - Company Overview

Northwind Analytics is a fictional B2B software company used as sample data
throughout these LangChain exercises. Nothing here describes a real business.

Founded in 2019 and headquartered in Pune, Northwind builds dashboards and
reporting tools for mid-sized logistics operators. The company employs around
180 people across Pune, Bengaluru, Chennai and Mumbai, and serves roughly 400
customers in India and South-East Asia.

Products

  Northwind Insight    A reporting dashboard for fleet and warehouse metrics.
                       The flagship product, used by about 90% of customers.
  Northwind Connect    An integration layer that pulls data from ERP systems,
                       telematics providers and spreadsheets.
  Northwind Alerts     A rules engine that notifies operations teams when a
                       shipment, vehicle or warehouse metric goes out of range.

Support model

Customers reach support through email and an in-app widget. Tickets are triaged
into six categories: billing, technical, account, cancellation or refund, order
and delivery, and general enquiries. Priority runs from low through critical.
A ticket is escalated to a human specialist when it involves a refund above
INR 50,000, a suspected data breach, or an outage affecting more than one
customer.

Support hours are 09:00 to 21:00 IST on weekdays, with an on-call rotation for
critical incidents outside those hours. The published first-response target is
four business hours for standard tickets and thirty minutes for critical ones.

Billing

Plans are billed annually in advance. The Starter plan covers up to 25 vehicles,
Growth up to 200, and Enterprise is priced per deployment. Customers may cancel
at any time; refunds are pro-rated from the start of the next billing month
rather than the cancellation date, which is the single most common source of
billing disputes.
