What to experiment with

  • justify-content: distributes items along the main axis.
  • align-items: aligns items across the cross axis.
  • flex-wrap: lets items move onto another line.
  • grid-template-columns: sets the grid's column structure.
  • gap: controls space between items in both layouts.
